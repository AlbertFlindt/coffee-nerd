# Code of conduct

Coffee Nerd is made by people who like coffee and care about details. Everyone is welcome here,
whatever their experience with espresso or with code, and whoever they are.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons, and accept that a decision can go
  the other way.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you got something wrong.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults, or remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, and personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after it was declined.

## When something goes wrong

Use **Report content** in the ··· menu of the comment, issue or pull request, and report it to
the repository; for abuse that breaks GitHub's own rules, report it to GitHub as well. Comments
that break this code are hidden or removed. Repeated or serious cases lead to a block from the
repository.

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and wherever someone
speaks for the project.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
